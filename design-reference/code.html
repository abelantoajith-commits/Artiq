Motion & Physics Rules

  • 120ms Button Press: Controls press 3px down & right with cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1) while shadow collapses completely to 0.
  • 280ms Masonry Rise-and-Fade: Single entrance animation with 40ms stagger per card. No ambient or looping animation to keep artwork quiet.
  • 150ms Chip Selection: Instant pastel fill shift with 2px hard shadow deployment.
  • A11y Prefers Reduced Motion: Bypasses all transitions and transforms to instant state changes.
